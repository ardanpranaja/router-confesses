# Candidates

Alternate lines considered for "The Router Confesses". The chosen line won because it gives the router a smug little secret and a petty grudge at the same time: it already knows the twist, and the ending is a reward the household has not earned. It dodges the obvious buffering, bandwidth and firmware-update jokes the judge has heard a hundred times.

1. "I didn't drop the connection — I just needed everyone in this room to finally look at me for once." — Strong router ego, but it is about attention, not about the movie; the excuse could fit any interruption, so it loses the climax.
2. "I saw the killer's face and decided none of you deserved to know yet." — Close second: same secret-keeping joke, but "decided" is flatter than "frankly none of you dust me", which gives the router its grudge.
3. "I already know who did it, and I'm keeping that secret until somebody dusts me for once." — Same idea as the chosen line, but "until somebody dusts me" makes the ransom explicit and slightly clunkier; the chosen line's "often enough to deserve the ending" lands the sting more cleanly.
4. "The climax was too much for me too — I fainted first, you're welcome." — Funny and dramatic, but fainting is just another way of saying "I crashed", and it gives the router no motive.
5. "I dropped out on purpose — the twist was so bad I was doing you a favour." — Good twist, but it insults the movie instead of making the router the character; the joke is about the film, not the router.

Chosen: "I know who the killer is, and frankly none of you dust me often enough to deserve the ending."
